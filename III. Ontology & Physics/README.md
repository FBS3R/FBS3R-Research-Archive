# III. Ontology & Physics

**FB(S³)R** · Fractal • Bindu • S³ • Reality

*An Interdisciplinary Research Programme*

> **Ontology • Epistemology • Philosophy of Physics**

This section explores the conceptual and physical foundations of the programme: what counts as existence, observation, emergence and a physical carrier. Its research areas and guiding question follow the **Foundations of Reality** section of the source framework.

## Research Areas

- Nature of the physical carrier
- Information
- Causality
- Physical distinguishability
- Observation
- Reality
- Emergence
- Physical existence

## Research Question

> Why do these questions arise at all, and what conceptual foundations make physical theories possible?
