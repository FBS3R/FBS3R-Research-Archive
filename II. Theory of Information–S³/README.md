**FB(S³)R** · Fractal • Bindu • S³ • Reality

*An Interdisciplinary Research Programme*

> **Information on Compact Physical Carriers**

This section examines the passage from formal distinctions to physically realised and operationally meaningful information.

## Research Areas

- Physical information
- Physical distinguishability
- Realised Physical Distinguishability (RPD)
- Information ontology
- Energy density
- State density
- Information actualisation
- Operational information

## Research Question

> How does information emerge from physically realised distinguishability on a compact carrier?
